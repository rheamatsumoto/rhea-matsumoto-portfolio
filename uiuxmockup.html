Project Overview

Designed for the course DES 112: UI/UX Principles, this project features desktop and mobile web interfaces for a platform offering free, creative activities for tweens (ages 10–12).

Tweens need engaging and creative spaces to explore individuality

The platform's mission is to foster self-expression and creativity through simple, accessible online activities.

Solution & Differentiation
